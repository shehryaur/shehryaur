## Builder Mode

### Seekho
AI lesson planning for Pakistani teachers.

Teachers choose class, subject, chapter, district, and language. Seekho creates a lesson pack with a teacher guide, student handout, activity, quiz, parent WhatsApp message, and weekly plan.

Why it exists: teachers do not need a flashy AI demo. They need tomorrow's lesson in their local context.

### MUV
A Telegram Mini App for coordinating spontaneous group movement.

Students can create or join walks, rides, pickup runs, and group pings. It supported 130+ concurrent users across 4 trip modes.

### ReproSafe
A local prototype for safer AI-assisted debugging.

It turns private data-processing bugs into reviewed synthetic reproducers so sensitive workflows can be debugged with more control.
